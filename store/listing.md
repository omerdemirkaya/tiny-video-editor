## English (en-us)

**Product name:** Tiny Video Editor

**Short description** (≤ 270 chars)
Cut, join and shrink videos in seconds. A light, multi-track video editor that runs smoothly even
on older computers — no account, no watermark, everything stays on your PC.

**Description**
Tiny Video Editor is a simple video editor for everyday jobs: cut out the boring parts, join clips,
add music, put text on a scene and make the file small enough to share.

Select a part of the timeline with the [ ] range, press S and it becomes its own clip. Delete it,
or give only that piece an effect, a caption, a different speed or volume. Video and sound sit on
separate tracks, so you can move, trim, mute or replace them independently. Add more videos as
layers (picture-in-picture) or extra audio tracks for music and voice-overs, and drag files
straight from a folder onto the timeline.

Press Play to watch the edited result with all effects before you export. When you are done,
export to MP4 (H.264 or H.265), MP3 or GIF. Pick a quality, or simply type the maximum file size
in MB — the app works out the rest. "Fast cut" saves pure cuts without re-encoding in seconds.

Built to be light: the whole project is rendered in a single pass by the included FFmpeg engine,
uses the graphics card when available and runs at low priority so your computer stays responsive.

No account. No watermark. No internet connection needed. Your videos never leave your computer.

**Features** (one per line, ≤ 200 chars each)
- Split with S: cut at the playhead or turn a selected [ ] range into its own clip
- Separate video and audio tracks; move clips between tracks and reorder tracks
- Per-clip effects: black & white, vintage, vivid, blur, sharpen, denoise, vignette and more
- Text captions, speed (0.25x–4x), rotate, mirror and picture-in-picture layouts
- Add music or voice-over on extra audio tracks; volume and mute per clip
- Live preview playback of the edited video
- Export MP4 (H.264 / H.265), MP3 or GIF; limit the file size in MB
- Fast cut without re-encoding; graphics card acceleration
- Change resolution, aspect ratio (9:16, 1:1, 4:5…), frame rate, fade in/out
- Undo / redo, drag & drop, works offline, no watermark

## Submission options → Restricted capabilities (runFullTrust) — justification

Tiny Video Editor is a classic desktop (Win32) application packaged with MSIX. It needs full trust
to run the bundled FFmpeg processes that decode, preview and encode the user's video files, and
to read and write video files in folders the user chooses. It does not access the network.

## Notes for certification

Tiny Video Editor is a free, offline video editor. To test: click "Choose a video", pick any MP4,
drag on the "Range" bar above the tracks, press S, then press Delete or change the clip in the
right panel; press Space to preview and "Export video…" to save. No account or sign-in is needed.
The app bundles FFmpeg 8.1 (GPL); its license is installed in the ffmpeg folder.
